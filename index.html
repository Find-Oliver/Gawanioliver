How to Use

Welcome to WorkNote by Oliver Plaza! 😊

This web app makes preparing your Monthly Accomplishment Report easier. Just follow these simple steps.

  1. Login: Use your email. Your notes are saved under your email account.
  2. Step 1 – Report Information: Enter the report title, office, reporting period, and Prepared by. Fields marked with (*) are required.
  3. Step 2 – Work Activities: Enter your activities, offices, quantities, and Total. Click = Sum to calculate automatically or Add Entry to add another activity.
  4. Check Your Entries: If the Total doesn't match the sum of the office quantities, a warning will appear. The app will not automatically change your Total.
  5. Step 3 – Table Format: Choose your table format, paper size, orientation, and margins. Click Create Table.
  6. Export or Print: Choose Export to Word (.docx), Copy to paste into Excel, or Print.
  7. Logout: Log out when finished, especially when using a shared computer.

Friendly Reminder: Take your time, double-check your information, and correct any mistakes before exporting or printing. You've got this! 👍
